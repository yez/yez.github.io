Common questions

How much does a small business website cost?
Most single-page business sites with booking land in the low four figures as a one-time build, with no monthly platform fee. Multi-page sites and ongoing SEO work are quoted separately. You get a fixed number before any work starts.
How long does it take to build?
A focused single-page site with booking typically takes two to three weeks from first call to launch. The slow part is usually gathering your photos, copy, and service details — the build itself is fast.
Who owns the domain and the website files?
You do. The domain is registered in your name on your account, and the site is plain HTML and CSS handed to you in a repository you control. There is no proprietary platform to get locked into and nothing stops working if we part ways.
Do I need a Google Business Profile?
If customers can visit you or you serve a defined area, yes — it is the single highest-leverage local SEO asset, and it is free. Setup and optimization is included in the local SEO work: categories, service areas, hours, photos, and matching schema on your site.
Can appointment booking connect to my existing calendar?
Yes. Booking connects to Google Calendar, Outlook, or iCloud so your real availability drives what customers can book. Confirmations, reminders, buffers between appointments, and intake questions are all configured as part of the setup.
Do you work with clients outside Richmond, Virginia?
Yes. Richmond-area clients can meet in person, and everything else is done remotely over email and video calls. The work is identical either way.
